Blog Organization

The blog is organized by "time" (ie, the day I decided to write) - I more or less just write what I feel - even if that means I span multiple topics in a single post or write an entire story/narrative/discourse/etc.

I know it's not the best organization method, but... It makes things easy for me! I plan to eventually organize content better, but for now, it's what I've got!
